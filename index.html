Subzero

Instruments for listening to, making and thinking about sound. Founded by Daniel Bovolon.

  • Planet Sound

    An open archive of the world's sound, with field recordings pinned to the place and time they were captured.

    ->
  • Understory

    A sound forest that grows in your browser, where trees and fungi talk in 3D sound with your clock and the seasons.

    ->
  • Wildtrack

    A daily brief for people who make sound: a seed idea, a photograph, a poem, something to listen to and news from every craft that meets audio.

    ->
  • Talkback

    A daily news brief for audio professionals, covering new tech, plugins and hardware, with a short lesson and a nine-question quiz every day.

    ->
  • Soundbay

    A toolkit for sound professionals: calculators for acoustics, timing and levels, plus a measurement bench, sketch pad, notebook and planner.

    ->
