Subzero

Instruments for listening to, making and thinking about sound. Founded by Daniel Bovolon.

  • Planet Sound

    The world, heard from where it happened: an open archive of field recordings, each pinned to its place and time.

    ->
  • Understory

    Plant a seed and listen as trees and fungi grow, trade sugar and signals, and fill a forest of 3D sound that follows your clock and the seasons.

    ->
  • Wildtrack

    Each morning brings a seed for your work: a real photograph, a poem, something to listen to and news from every craft that touches sound.

    ->
  • Talkback

    News from the control room, every day: new tech, plugins and hardware for audio professionals, plus a short lesson and a nine-question quiz.

    ->
  • Soundbay

    Calculate, measure, sketch and plan in one place, with acoustics, timing and level tools built for working sound engineers.

    ->
